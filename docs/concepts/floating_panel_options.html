Floating Composer Redesign Options

We reimagined the "Local Change" modal to prioritize clarity and speed, removing generic headers and focusing purely on actionable intents. Below are 5 radically different approaches to the same functionality.

1. The Spotlight (Input-First)

Inverts the hierarchy. Instead of forcing you to choose a tab first, you just type what you want done. Chips below act as quick templates or mode limits.

2. Pill & Context Menus

Extremely compact. The floating element is just a small pill. Clicking an action drops down a minimal popover for secondary inputs (like prompts or options).

3. Refined iOS-style Card

Similar to the current logic, but greatly cleaned up. Heavy title headers are removed. Tabs use a smooth segmented control. Layout is visually balanced and borders are softer.

Запит вище використовується лише для режиму «Правка за запитом».

4. Notion-style Command List

A vertical flat list of commands. No tabs, no hidden states. Everything is immediately visible and clickable. Excellent for power-users who want zero friction.

5. Minimal Vertical Sidebar

Saves horizontal space for text. The left strip handles mode switching with clean, unlabelled icons. The right pane adjusts dynamically based on the chosen mode.
